# What's new in Next Up

The short version, newest first. CHANGELOG.md has every detail.

## 0.2.5 — 28 October 2026

- Channels is now a proper TV guide. What's on now sits up top as cards with progress bars, then a grid of every channel across the next few hours with a red line for now. Hover anything to see what it is, with the episode's own picture and blurb.
- Filter the guide by Everything, Films or TV, and it remembers which you picked.
- Every channel has its own colour in the guide, so no two rows next to each other look the same, and what's on now lights up in it.
- Hovering an On now card doesn't just show you the same picture again: it's the blurb and what's on next instead.
- Channels' top is all one line now: Everything, Films, TV, Shuffle and Remote on the left, the main tabs on the right.
- Remote opens the remote and now closes it too.
- Everything on that top line sits level now.
- The now playing bar looks the same and sits in the same spot on every page, and there's only ever one Remote button.
- Reorder your channels: click a channel's number in the guide, type a new one, hit Enter, and it moves there.
- When a block on Channels is a few episodes back to back, there's a line between each one, and hovering shows the episode that's actually on, not just the first.
- Directors Cut and Type Cast now show whose films you're watching, with their name over their run.
- On the phone, Channels shows each channel's now and next two, with HERE and ON PC.
- Shows can be put right like films: WRONG MATCH? on a show's page looks it up again and fetches its episodes fresh.
- The year in a show's folder name now picks between shows with the same name, so One Piece is the anime, not the Netflix one.
- That now goes for shows already in the library too, so One Piece finally stops being the Netflix one, and Planet Earth stops pretending to be The Living Planet.
- Anime numbered straight through (One Piece episode 830 and all that) now finds its episode details.
- "I'm up to here" no longer makes the later episodes look watched, and it updates the season list in place rather than flashing the whole page.
- History counts a watch as a minute or more that you ended yourself. If the sleep timer switched it off, it doesn't count.
- The bar along the top of the window is solid now, so nothing shows through it when you scroll a page up underneath.

## 0.2.4 — 27 October 2026

- Ratings, a new place in the Library (the ★), with a Hall of Fame: everything you've rated as shelves, from your top 12 to the decades and the Kubrick shelf.
- Rate lives in Ratings now too, so the top menu is just Now · Library · Channels.
- Watch in order: story shows like Arcane only get suggested at the episode you're up to; dip-in ones like Seinfeld can come up anywhere. A purple arrow or shuffle on the poster says which.
- Films tags on Now: pick Film and a second row opens with things like Cult classic, Mind-bending, True story, Box office flop, Short and sweet and Never seen. They mix with the genres and each other.
- Now's tags sit grey until you press them, then light up in their own colours, and they stay put rather than shuffling about as you choose.
- Click an episode's code on a show's page to say "I'm up to here", handy for a show you started somewhere else.
- A gold trophy on a film's Library poster when it tops Hall of Fame shelves, with how many (2001: A Space Odyssey ×4).
- The TMDB score is off the Now card, so it doesn't sway the choice.
- Continue and History only count proper watching now: quick flicks and stops after a minute or two are left out, and a show you barely started still turns up as up next.
- On Windows the window behaves itself at last: it resizes, maximises without covering the taskbar, and the odd grey strip along the top is gone. Plus a handful of small fixes.

## 0.2.2 — 25 October 2026

- Home: the Library's new front page and where the app opens. Quick Suggest offers the better episodes of shows you like and films you love that you haven't seen lately, plus a few wildcards, and Continue sits under it, TV and films together. Both rows always fit the window.
- Each season and episode on a show's page has a score, with a ★ on the show's five best and a mark on the unseen ones that should be good. Click a score to rate the episode yourself.
- Click an episode's name for its details: the pic, plot, air date, director, writers and guest stars.
- Closing Next Up on the PC tucks it into the system tray, so the phone keeps working.
- The phone's Remote, with nothing playing, gives you somewhere to start: carry on on the PC, what's on the channels, or one of tonight's picks.
- A new Silver look and a Font choice (Next Up, Sleek, Cinema) in Settings › Appearance.
- Now on the PC sits higher with no scroll bar, long titles shrink rather than get cut off, and the line above the rating tells you one thing worth knowing ("You gave 2001: A Space Odyssey a 9.5").
- The PC's now-playing bar has its own controls, and the show page is wider with the cast down the left.
- The phone's Now page is redesigned to fit on one screen, with the buttons always in the same places.
- Channels reshuffled: some are films only, Non-stop Favs splits into Films and TV, Cold Ones is gone. Plus fixes so channel flicking and quick closes don't clutter Continue and History, and films with the wrong year in their folder match properly.

## Before 0.1 — 4 August 2026

The early days, before the numbering started.

- **Channels:** twelve of them plus a live news channel, all on a fixed schedule.
- **Library:** paged, with half-star ratings, a Watchlist, and Recently added that works for shows.
- **Pages:** film pages with the plot, cast and similar films from your shelf; episodes with their real names and pictures; and a way to fix a wrong match.
- **Picks:** the evening's picks stop being nearly all TV, and well-rated films come back as rewatches.
- **Everything else:** a now-playing bar on every page, Up Next renamed Recommendations, ratings and history export from Settings, and the Next Up logo as the icon.
